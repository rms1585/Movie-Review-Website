Limereel: logo concepts v0.1

Three rough directions built on one idea: a lime slice that is also a film reel. The lime's segments double as the reel's cut-outs. Every mark is one flat colour, so it works on light and dark backgrounds and at tiny sizes. These are concepts to choose a direction from. A designer refines the chosen one into the final logo.

Idea

A lime cut across: the rind becomes the reel's rim, the six segments become the reel's cut-outs, and the core becomes the hub. You see a lime first, then the reel.

Strengths

  • Simple, so it survives at 16px (browser tab icon).
  • Uses the lime brand colour naturally.
  • Works on its own, as an app icon or as an avatar.

Risks

  • At a glance it reads more "fruit" than "film". It needs the name next to it early on.
  • Round, segmented marks are common (Chrome, citrus brands). A designer should make the segment shapes unmistakably reel-like.

B · Unspooling lockup

Symbol + wordmark
One colour
On After Hours

Idea

The slice-reel from A, with a strip of film running out from its base and becoming the line under the name. It says "film" without words.

Strengths

  • Reads as film immediately, which fixes A's "just fruit" risk.
  • A natural animation for the site: the strip unspools on first load.

Risks

  • Wide, so it only fits headers and the footer. A is still needed for small places.
  • The film strip gets busy below about 120px wide. Drop it at small sizes.

C · Wordmark with slice dot

Wordmark
One colour
On After Hours

Idea

The name does the work. The only symbol is a tiny lime slice dotting the "i". It's quiet, confident and editorial.

Strengths

  • The name is memorised from day one, which matters most for a new brand.
  • Easiest to execute well, and the slice dot can grow into the A symbol later.

Risks

  • The slice is tiny here, so the film idea is mostly lost.
  • It relies on the typeface. This uses Archivo from design.md, and a custom-drawn wordmark would be stronger.

Before this becomes the real logo

  1. Direction chosen: the horizontal lockup (symbol A + wordmark side by side, tight spacing), with A alone for favicons, app icons and avatars.
  2. Check the name: domain (limereel.com / .app / .film), social handles and a trademark search.
  3. Designer refinement: optical balance, making the segment shapes more reel-like, custom letter spacing, and exporting SVG, PNG and favicon sets.
